description: |
  Take a set of published content items out of public view — inventory, health-check, pick the least damaging mechanism the accounts can actually use, execute, prove it guest-side.
  Do NOT use for permanent deletion, for renaming or fixing item metadata, or for retesting a bug fix (retest-bug).

Read and follow [the content-takedown workflow](../skills/content-takedown-workflow/SKILL.md) end-to-end.

Pass arguments after `/content-takedown` as the batch definition — a content type, a filter, a list
of ids, or a description such as "the recorded livestreams from the training round". If none, ask
which set to hide.

Confirm the environment and the account set in one message and wait for the answer before any tool
call. Show the batch — titles and count — before touching it.

Work down the mechanism ladder and stop at the first rung a real call proves available; the
report-based rung writes a permanent accusation against a named creator and needs the user's
informed approval restated after they hear what it says about them.

Finish with the five-layer guest-side verification and send the guest screenshot in chat. State the
rollback call and the ids it needs.

Follow [references/user-communication.md](../references/user-communication.md).
